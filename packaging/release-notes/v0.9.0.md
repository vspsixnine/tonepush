TonePush 0.9.0 puts your library under the editor: click a tone to hear it on the pedal without writing anything, then keep it or put the preset back. Tones, presets, setlists and files drag to where they go, and you can publish to TonePush for everyone or only for yourself. The StompStation PRO saves on firmware 2.2, its 2.x chain is drawn and edited with parallel paths and free positions, and Windows finds it. Windows also gets an installer.

## New

- **The library sits under the editor.** Tones, Setlists and Cloud share a pane along the bottom that you can resize or fold (Ctrl L), so the preset stays in view. The pedal's backups, impulse responses, NAM models and settings open from its card in the sidebar. By @crmne.
- **Click a tone to hear it.** It plays in the loaded preset without writing the pedal's memory, and the preset waits with its unsaved changes. **Keep in 01B** (Enter) keeps the tone, **Put back** (Esc) brings the preset back, and the arrow keys step through the tones. Knobs you turn while trying a tone stay with the tone. TonePush's tones, a setlist's slots and older versions play the same way. By @crmne.
- **Drag things to where they go.** Tones onto a preset, the board, a setlist's slot or the Cloud tab; presets onto the Tones tab; setlists onto the presets; tone files from outside onto the Tones tab or a preset. A drag copies. Every row's right-click menu has the same order, with its keyboard shortcuts. By @crmne.
- **TonePush asks before replacing a preset.** Putting a tone in a slot that holds a preset says what goes and where it will still be; an empty slot is written at once. Several tones go in one step. By @crmne.
- **Every tone says which pedal it is for**: the HX model, or the firmware a StompStation PRO tone needs (1.5 or 2.x). A tone the connected pedal cannot play is marked, with the reason. By @crmne.
- **Publishing asks first, and your tones are listed.** The publish sheet shows the song, the tone, the pedal and who can see it: **Everyone** or **Only you**. A new version goes to the tone's existing song instead of a new one. **Mine** lists every tone and setlist you published, from any computer, and you can hide, rename or delete them there. Plays on TonePush are counted apart from downloads. By @crmne.
- **The StompStation PRO's 2.x chain is drawn and edited.** The board shows the chain as the pedal runs it: fourteen positions, fixed blocks locked, free positions as slots, and parallel blocks as lanes. Add a block from a free position, swap blocks by dragging, and join or split parallel paths. `tonepush pro set` sets the chain from the command line. Checked on a pedal at 2.0.10 by reading back each change. By @crmne.
- **The StompStation PRO saves on firmware 2.2.** TonePush now sends 2.2 smaller requests with short pauses, which keeps the pedal answering over USB even after your computer has used it as a sound card. Importing, renaming, moving, saving, clearing, IRs, NAM models, settings and restore were each checked on a pedal at 2.2.6 by reading back the result. A full backup of a 2.2 pedal takes about six minutes. By @crmne.
- **Patch releases of the StompStation PRO firmware count as the release they patch**, so a pedal on 1.5.10 or 2.0.8 saves like 1.5.12 or 2.0.10. On firmware TonePush does not know yet, a block's knobs can still be turned. By @crmne.
- **A Windows installer**, for x64 and ARM. It needs no administrator rights, adds TonePush to the Start menu, installs the `tonepush` command-line tool, and updates by running the next installer. The zip remains for running without installing. By @crmne.
- **Scrolling matches other apps:** a wheel notch moves further, and on Linux a touchpad glides on after you lift your fingers. By @crmne.

## Fixed

- **Windows finds a StompStation PRO.** Windows' own serial driver does not report the pedal's name, so 0.8.0 never found it there. TonePush now asks the port who it is. By @crmne.
- **The editor keeps moving while a StompStation PRO backs up**, its progress updates as it goes, and deleting many tones from the library at once no longer freezes the window. By @crmne.
- **A tone that cannot be played no longer leaves "Loading" on screen for good.** If the pedal goes, or you delete the tone while it waits, the audition ends and says why. By @crmne.
- **Paste and dropping files work together on Hyprland**, so dropping a WAV, a preset or the HX Edit installer on the window works on Wayland again. By @crmne.
- **The editor starts on old Windows graphics drivers**, and on Macs with a Touch Bar a closed window no longer lingers. By @crmne.
- **A button's shortcut sits on the same line as its label.** By @crmne.

## Known limitations

- NAM models cannot be reordered on firmware 2.0.x, because that firmware keeps playing the old model until it restarts.
- An HX Effects backup can still time out while reading the pedal's settings (#9), and an HX Stomp on firmware 3.80 that stays on "transferring data" (#5) is still being investigated.
- A copy unpacked from the Windows zip keeps updating as a zip. Install with the setup program once to switch.
